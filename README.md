**Description:**

### 💡 What is Attrition, Really?  
In business terms, *attrition* refers to the rate at which employees leave the company. A high attrition rate doesn’t just mean people are quitting — it’s often a sign of deeper workplace issues like low engagement, uncompetitive pay, or limited growth opportunities.  

With rising job mobility and changing workforce expectations, tracking attrition is no longer optional — it’s critical.
